MCA Viva Voce & Project Defense Guide

Key technical concepts to explain during the practical examination and project presentation:

1. How does TF-IDF feature extraction work in this project? ▼
TF-IDF (Term Frequency - Inverse Document Frequency) converts raw text strings into numerical vectors.
• TF measures the frequency of a word within a single document: TF(t, d) = count(t, d) / total_words(d).
• IDF penalizes common words that appear across all documents (like 'the', 'said') while rewarding distinctive words: IDF(t) = log((1 + N) / (1 + df(t))) + 1.
• In our pipeline, we configured n-gram ranges (1, 2) (unigrams and bigrams) and sublinear_tf=True (applying logarithmic scaling to term frequencies 1 + log(tf)) with 25,000 max features.
2. Why did LinearSVC outperform Multinomial Naive Bayes? ▼
• Naive Bayes assumption: MultinomialNB assumes that all features (words) are conditionally independent given the class label. In human language, word pairs and syntax have strong mutual correlations, which violates this independence assumption.
• LinearSVC (Support Vector Machine): LinearSVC finds the optimal maximum-margin hyperplane separating real news and fake news in the high-dimensional TF-IDF space (25,000 dimensions). Text classification problems are well-known to be linearly separable, making LinearSVC exceptionally effective and robust against sparse data.
3. How did we guarantee NO data leakage during preprocessing? ▼
• Data Leakage Prevention: The TfidfVectorizer was fitted strictly and solely on X_train using vectorizer.fit_transform(X_train).
• The test set X_test was transformed using only vectorizer.transform(X_test) without calling fit().
• This ensures that vocabulary frequencies and IDF weights from unseen test articles never leaked into the training process.
• Furthermore, Reuters news agency wire prefixes were cleaned to prevent the models from memorizing publisher source headers.
4. What do Precision, Recall, and F1-Score signify in Fake News Detection? ▼
• Precision (TP / (TP + FP)): Out of all articles predicted as Fake News, how many were genuinely fake? High precision prevents real news from being falsely flagged as fake (avoiding censorship).
• Recall (TP / (TP + FN)): Out of all actual fake news articles, how many did the system catch? High recall prevents misinformation from slipping through undetected.
• F1-Score: Harmonic mean of Precision and Recall: 2 × (Precision × Recall) / (Precision + Recall). It serves as our primary model selection criterion because it balances both false positives and false negatives.
5. Why use Joblib instead of Pickle for model serialization? ▼
• joblib is an optimized replacement for Python's standard pickle module that handles large NumPy arrays and sparse SciPy matrices (such as TF-IDF feature matrices and LinearSVC weight arrays) with zero-copy memory mapping and fast compression.
